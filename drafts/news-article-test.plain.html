Arrowhead Intermediaries Appoints Leah Berger as Chief Counsel

July 28, 2026

San Diego, CA — Arrowhead Intermediaries today announced the appointment of Leah Berger as Chief Counsel, where she will lead legal strategy across the organization's global specialty insurance platform.

Berger brings more than 20 years of legal experience spanning insurance and reinsurance, specialty products, corporate transactions, governance, regulatory matters, privacy and cybersecurity compliance.

About Arrowhead Intermediaries

Arrowhead Intermediaries is a diverse platform for the global insurance marketplace, operating three distinct divisions: Programs, Wholesale, and Specialty.
